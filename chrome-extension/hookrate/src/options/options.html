Hookrate settings

Installed

Open YouTube and the panels appear on their own: a channel-analytics card on any channel page, a stats card in the watch sidebar, a metrics overlay on Shorts, a filter bar on the home feed, and per-row annotations on search results.

Features

Everything runs locally. Turn off what you do not want injected.

Revenue model

Revenue is estimated as views ÷ 1000 × category RPM × geography multiplier. No external service publishes another channel's real RPM, so these are benchmarks — override them with numbers from your own dashboard for accuracy in your niche.

Tracker

How often tracked channels are re-polled while Chrome is running.

Data and privacy

Lookups are made from the extension's background worker to youtube.com with cookies omitted, so your signed-in session is never attached to research calls. Nothing is sent to any third-party server — there is no Hookrate backend. Cached results and your swipe file live in local extension storage only.
