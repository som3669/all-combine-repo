The idea

Some criteria only exist after a click

WCAG 3.3.1 and 3.3.3 describe error messages that must be announced, marked on the failing field, and specific enough to act on. Those states do not exist until a customer submits a form — so no static scan reaches them, at any price, however many rules it ships.

Because the scan drives a real browser, Pro can perform the interaction first and then check the result. That is the difference between testing a shop and scanning a page, and it is the reason this add-on exists.

No order is ever created. The checkout is emptied before submission and the emptiness is then verified; if any required field still holds a value, the check aborts rather than clicking Place Order.

What is in it

What Pro adds

  • Checkout error testing — submits the form empty and checks the errors are announced to a screen reader, marked on the failing fields, linked to them, and specific enough to act on
  • Keyboard purchase path — add to cart, proceed to checkout and place order each reachable and operable with no mouse, with focus visibly obvious
  • Add-to-cart feedback — whether adding a product from the keyboard tells the customer it worked
  • Full-store coverage — up to 50 products, categories, pages and posts each, with products sampled across categories rather than by date
  • Template grouping — the same missing label on 400 product pages is one theme fix, and the report leads with whichever fix repairs the most pages
  • Scheduled scans — daily, weekly or monthly, with an email when something gets worse
  • Scan comparison — fixed, newly detected and unchanged since last time, because regressions arrive through updates rather than anything you did
  • A dated audit report — standard tested, tool versions, priority fixes, findings by page, exclusions with reasons, testing history, signature block; print-optimised for PDF
  • An accessibility statement — published following the EU model structure, its known-limitations section built from your latest scan so it stays true
  • An ignore log — record findings you assessed and chose not to act on, with a mandatory reason, printed in the report
  • Bulk alt text — every image missing alt text in one screen, with the page it is attached to as a hint; nothing written for you

Honesty

What it is not

The audit report is a self-evaluation using automated testing, and it says so on its own face. That is real evidence of diligence, and it is usually what a client or an insurer wants to see.

It is not an independent audit and not a conformance certificate. If you need one of those you need a qualified human evaluator, and no plugin at any price changes that. Automated testing reaches about a third of WCAG; Pro reaches more of it than free does, and documents the rest.

Questions

Asked before buying

What happens when my licence expires?

Paid features stop unlocking. Your scan history, ignore log and settings are left completely alone — that record is your evidence and it stays yours.

Does it replace the free plugin?

No, it requires it. The free plugin owns the scanner and the database; Pro extends it through documented hooks.

Will the checkout test create test orders?

No. The form is emptied and the emptiness verified before anything is submitted; if a required field still holds a value the check aborts rather than placing an order.

Will it break when the free plugin updates?

The two update independently and are checked against an extension API version rather than matching version numbers. If they are ever incompatible, Pro pauses with an admin notice rather than causing a fatal error.
