AI Patro – Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

AI Patro ("we", "our", or "us") operates the AI Patro mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

Information We Do Not Collect

AI Patro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App works entirely offline. No account registration is required.

Data Stored on Your Device

The App stores only your selected calendar date locally on your device using Flutter's standard state management. This data never leaves your device.

Internet Permission

The App declares the INTERNET permission in its manifest to support potential future features. As of the current version, no network requests are made.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under 13. The App is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
